# The reference, measured

A 60.0 s launch film for a fictional shop builder, 1920x1080 at 30 fps, stereo 48 kHz. Measured on 2026-10-28:
contact sheets every 0.5 s, 10 fps bursts around every join, a per-frame scene-change score, and the audio's beat
grid (`beatgrid.py`: 120.0 BPM, 113 of 117 beats measured, every beat within 1 frame of its onset, key D major at
confidence 1.0) and band energies per bar. Its words, brand and pictures are the reference's own: never put them
in a reel.

the picture, not from volume rides.

## The cuts

From the per-frame scene-change score: big changes at 8.0, 12.0-16.0 every 0.5 s, 15.5, 22.0, 48.0, 52.4-53.9
(the slam) and 53.27. Everything else is a continuous move (a zoom, a collapse, a swirl, a whip), so a plain cut
detector sees the reel as 8 shots: the joins are motion, not cuts. Every change sits on the beat grid within a frame.
